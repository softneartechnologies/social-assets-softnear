Picture one hotel running one Instagram ad, for the same room at the same price, sending half the clicks to website A and half to website B.

On website A, the guest lands on a photo gallery, a welcome note from the owner and a "Call for rates" button.

On website B, the guest lands on that room, picks dates, sees the price per night and a Book button. A WhatsApp button sits next to it for anyone with a question.

Only one thing changed between the two: whether the rate and the button are on the first screen.

We'd bet on website B, and we build hotel sites that way every time. Someone who tapped an ad is already interested. Asking them to call for the price hands them back to Booking.com or MakeMyTrip, already open on the same phone.

Our team has a line for this. A website is the net, and ads, Google and Instagram are the water. You need both, and an ad budget pointed at a website that hides the rate is money thrown in the water.

When we look at a hotel or homestay website, we check four things first: the rate on the first screen, real room photos with the location, a clear cancellation policy, and a booking engine that works on a phone.
