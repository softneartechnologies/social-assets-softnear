## Voice

- Plain, confident, specific. Sounds like a senior engineer explaining a decision to a founder, not like an agency brochure.
- "We" for the company. Short paragraphs, one idea each.
- Contractions are fine. British/Indian-neutral English ("organise" or "organize" is fine, be consistent within a post).
- Emoji: none on LinkedIn; at most one on Instagram/Facebook, as punctuation.
- Hashtags: LinkedIn 0-2, Instagram up to 5 specific ones, Facebook 0-3.
- Words we never use: delve, leverage, robust, seamless, cutting-edge, game-changer, revolutionise, synergy, unlock, empower, "in today's fast-paced world", "elevate", "next-level", "one-stop solution".
- Positions we hold (good for opinion posts):
  1. Most software projects fail on unclear scope, not bad code.
  2. Version one should be smaller than the client wants.
  3. AI belongs where a process repeats, not where a demo impresses.
  4. A dedicated developer you can trial for 7 days beats a long hiring loop.
  5. Agencies should never have to explain to their client who actually wrote the code.
